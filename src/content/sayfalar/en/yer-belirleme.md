  - baslik: "How do you choose the right locating system?"
    metin: |-
      Choosing the locating system starts with three questions: can the route be walked at the surface, how deep and how long is the crossing, and what accuracy is required? For urban, short and medium-distance HDD projects, [walk-over locating](/en/walk-over-locating/) is sufficient and economical. On long and deep routes under rivers, straits, motorways or plants, where there is no surface access, the [magnetic guidance](/en/magnetic-guidance/) system is the only option. In areas dense with interference sources — high-voltage lines, steel structures, metro lines — the wireline system also gives more reliable results.

      **Deltek** operates both systems with its own crews and has the experience of hitting the target on crossings of more than 2,000 metres with Trutracker® and Paratrack® magnetic systems. The choice of system and its limits are shared with the client at the tender stage, and the bore profile is designed within those limits.
