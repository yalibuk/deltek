## Request a quote for your HDD project

Deltek provides **horizontal directional drilling (HDD)**, **pipe jacking and
auger boring / microtunnelling** and **trenchless pipe bursting** across Türkiye
from its Istanbul and Izmir offices. For river, motorway, railway and urban
crossings of natural gas, water, sewer, power and fibre-optic lines, our project
department determines the most suitable trenchless method, rig class and crew
once it has the ground information, crossing length and pipe diameter — **the
feasibility study and site survey are free of charge.**

The following details speed up your quotation: crossing location (province and,
if possible, coordinates), approximate length, pipe material and diameter, a
geotechnical report if available, and the planned schedule. You can reach us by
phone or e-mail; we reply the same working day.

## Where we work

Our head office is in Izmir and our branch is in Istanbul; our crews and fleet
mobilise to every region of Türkiye. We have completed horizontal directional
drilling and pipe jacking projects in the Marmara, Aegean, Mediterranean,
Central Anatolia, Black Sea, Eastern and South-Eastern Anatolia regions, and we
are experienced in international projects as well.

## Frequently asked questions

**How is the price of an HDD crossing determined?** Crossing length, pipe
diameter and material, ground conditions, depth and the type of locating system
determine the price. Rather than a flat rate per metre, we prepare a
project-specific quotation. Details: [what determines the cost](/en/horizontal-drilling-cost/).

**How soon can you start?** After the feasibility study and site survey, and
provided permits are in place, we can mobilise to most urban HDD projects within
two weeks.

**Is a geotechnical survey mandatory?** Short, shallow crossings can proceed on
existing data; for river, motorway and long crossings we require a geotechnical
survey. If you do not have one, we can organise it with the geotechnical teams
we trust.
