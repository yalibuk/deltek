Deltek is a trenchless contractor with more than 20 years of field experience,
providing **horizontal directional drilling**, **pipe jacking and auger boring**
and **trenchless pipe bursting** across Türkiye from its Istanbul and Izmir
offices. With a fleet of up to 250 tonnes in HDD and 800 tonnes in pipe jacking,
we install pipe and cable lines of every diameter from 2 mm to 2000 mm under
rivers, motorways, railways and city streets without open-cut excavation. We
carry out a free feasibility and site survey for each of the four services below.
