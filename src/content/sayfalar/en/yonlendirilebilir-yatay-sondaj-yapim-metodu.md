  - baslik: "Summary of the method and planning"
    metin: |-
      The horizontal directional drilling construction method consists of three main stages: the **pilot bore**, **reaming** and **pullback**. During the pilot bore, the sonde inside the steering head allows the depth, inclination and direction of the bit to be monitored continuously; when the path deviates from the design profile, the rig stops rotating and pushes only, turning the head in the required direction. Reaming is carried out in stages until the hole reaches 1.2–1.5 times the pipe diameter; at each stage the drilling fluid both carries the cuttings out and keeps the hole stable. During pullback the pipe is connected to the reamer through a swivel; because the swivel does not transfer the reamer's rotation to the pipe, the pipe is not twisted.

      Site planning is an inseparable part of the method. Entry and exit angles are usually chosen between 8° and 18°, depending on the rig class and pipe material; the allowable bending radius of the pipeline determines the deepest point of the profile and its total length. No route is designed without a geotechnical survey, the groundwater level and a map of existing utilities. **Deltek** shares this profile with the client on every HDD project; rig capacity, drill rod set, reaming stages and mud recipe are all determined from the ground data. See also the [HDD rig selection](/en/yonlendirilebilir-yatay-sondaj-makinesi/) and [drilling fluid](/en/yatay-sondaj-camuru/) pages.
