Trenchless drilling technologies are the methods of installing pipe and cable
lines underground without opening a trench at the surface. In this section we
describe the four methods Deltek applies in the field and the main components of
**horizontal directional drilling (HDD)** one by one: what HDD is, the
construction method and rig classes; drill rods, steering heads and reamers;
walk-over and magnetic locating systems; drilling fluid. The other trenchless
methods are pipe jacking / pipe ramming (pipe pushing, auger boring,
microtunnelling), trenchless pipe bursting and the smart undergrounding
approach. The topic guides are long-form pages that explain horizontal drilling
and trenchless boring from the ground up.
